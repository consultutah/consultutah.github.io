Lewiston Planner Support

A calm, local-only daily planner for iPhone and iPad.

Plan your day

Use the plus buttons to add priorities and appointments. Assign A, B, or C priorities, set recurrence, and check off completed tasks. Unfinished tasks appear on today’s page. Tap the date to choose another day.

Notes and wider layouts

Use Plan and Notes on compact screens. With enough available width, your plan and notes appear side by side.

Appearance and backups

Open Settings for Light, Dark, or Match System. Export a backup to Files regularly. Restore imports a Lewiston JSON backup and replaces your current planner. Lewiston has no automatic cross-device sync or reminder notifications.

Get help

Email jeff@consultutah.com with your device model, iOS version, and a description of the problem. Avoid sending private planner entries.
