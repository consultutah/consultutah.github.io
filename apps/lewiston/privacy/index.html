Lewiston Planner Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Lewiston Planner is a local-only planner from ConsultUtah LLC. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your planner entries. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics SDK, or developer-operated server.

Your planner

Tasks, appointments, notes, tracker text, and appearance preferences are stored on your device. The app does not automatically sync your planner between devices.

Backups you control

You can export a JSON backup through Files. The location you choose may be on your device or with a cloud storage provider. That provider handles the exported file under its own privacy terms. Protect backup files because they contain your planner entries. Restoring a backup replaces the current planner; the app retains a local pre-restore recovery copy.

Device backups and deletion

Apple device backups may include app data according to your device settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Delete any separately exported backups from their storage locations when no longer needed.

Support

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond. Please do not send sensitive planner contents. Contact jeff@consultutah.com with privacy or support questions.
