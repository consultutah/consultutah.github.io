DUOM Support

Get into a game

Choose a campaign, starting mission, difficulty and co-op or deathmatch mode, then tap Deploy. FreeDM is for deathmatch. Both players use the same device in landscape orientation.

Controls

Each half has its own movement and look sticks. Fire shoots, Use opens doors or activates switches, and weapon controls change weapons. Use Pause to take a break. Respawn returns a defeated player to the action.

Audio and maps

The initial release includes sound effects but no background music. Bundled content needs no downloads. Optional WAD import is for files you have the right to use; compatibility with every external WAD or mod is not guaranteed.

Contact

Email jeff@consultutah.com. Include your device model, iOS version, campaign and map, and what happened. Please do not send passwords or private files.
