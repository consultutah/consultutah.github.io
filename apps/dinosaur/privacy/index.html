Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026. This policy covers Dinosaur Letters Explorer for iPhone and iPad, provided by ConsultUtah LLC.

What stays on your device

The app does not require an account or collect your name, email, child’s name, location, contacts, photos, or microphone recordings. Drawings, discovery badges, and purchase entitlements are stored locally. We do not operate an app backend, use advertising or analytics SDKs, track users across apps, or sell personal data.

Bundled offline narration

All 60 story recordings are included in the app and play offline. The recordings were generated using xAI during production from our authored story text, before distribution. The app does not connect to xAI, download speech models or voices, send story text to a speech service, or send user data for AI processing. Earlier test builds downloaded model files; current builds remove those obsolete files on upgrade.

Purchases

Apple processes optional one-time purchases and restores through the App Store. We do not receive payment-card details. The app checks Apple-signed transactions to unlock content. Apple’s own privacy and payment terms apply.

Children

The app is designed for children with grown-up supervision. It has no child accounts, chat, advertisements, or public sharing. Purchases and external support/privacy links are behind a parental gate. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should handle support requests.

Backups and deletion

Your device’s Apple backup settings may include app data. Deleting the app removes its local drawings, badges, and other app data; Apple may retain backups or purchase records under its policies. Purchases can be restored using the purchasing Apple Account. No account-deletion request is needed because the app has no accounts.

Support and this website

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use it only to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send children’s personal information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed for support or legal obligations; contact us to request deletion. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose hosting logs may include connection information. We add no advertising or tracking cookies. GitHub controls hosting logs and their retention and storage locations under its privacy statement.

Contact and updates

Questions or privacy requests: jeff@consultutah.com. We will update this page and its effective date if these practices change.
