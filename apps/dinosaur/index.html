Dinosaur Letters Explorer

Explore 60 dinosaurs through illustrated letters, field notes, creative missions, and read-aloud stories on iPhone and iPad.

Start with T. rex for free. Additional dinosaurs are optional one-time purchases: US $0.99 each or US $19.99 for the full library. No subscription. Local App Store prices apply. Individual purchases are not credited toward the full-library purchase.

No ads, chat, or child accounts. Narration is generated on your device after a one-time voice download (about 342 MB). Stories, activities, and downloaded voices work offline.

Dinosaur Letters Explorer is currently in TestFlight and awaiting App Store review.
