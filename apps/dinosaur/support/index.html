Dinosaur Support

Parents and guardians: email jeff@consultutah.com with the app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a brief description. Do not include a child’s name or other personal information.

Listening

Tap Listen on an unlocked dinosaur. The first use automatically downloads approximately 342 MB of voice files. Keep the app open; Wi-Fi is recommended. Once downloaded, voices work offline. Check the media volume and your selected headphone or speaker output if audio is quiet. Stop and tap Listen to retry after a network error.

Purchases and restore

T. rex is free. A grown-up can unlock individual dinosaurs or the full library with a one-time App Store purchase. Use Parent corner → Restore purchases with the same Apple Account to recover purchases. For billing or refund requests, use Apple’s purchase support.

Drawings and badges

Drawings and discovery badges are saved on this device. Updating the app preserves app data. Do not delete and reinstall as a troubleshooting step unless you are comfortable losing local drawings and badges.
