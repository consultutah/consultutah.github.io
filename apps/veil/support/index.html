Veil Support

Help with readings and credits.

Before your first reading

Use an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone running iOS 27. Enable Apple Intelligence and allow its required model to download. Veil will explain when the model is unavailable. Saved readings remain readable without the model.

Taking a photo

Use soft, even lighting, keep your palm open, and include the whole palm in focus. If a photo cannot be read, try a clearer image. Failed generation or saving does not consume a reading credit.

Credits and purchases

Your first reading is free. Optional packs add 5, 15, or 40 readings; Apple shows local prices before purchase. There is no subscription. If a purchase is delayed, use Check pending purchases. This recovers unfinished transactions; it is not a cross-device restore feature. Credits are held in a device-only wallet, and recovery after reinstall or device replacement is not guaranteed.

Contact support

Email jeff@consultutah.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, Veil version, and a description of the problem. Do not send payment-card details. Palm readings are symbolic entertainment, not predictions or professional advice.
