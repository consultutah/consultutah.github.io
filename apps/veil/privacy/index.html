Veil Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy describes Veil version 1.0 build 14 and later on-device versions provided by Consult Utah.

Palm photos and readings

Veil uses a photo you choose or take to generate a symbolic reading and ink-style artwork on your iPhone. These photos, generated readings, and artwork are stored locally with iOS file protection. Veil does not upload them to Consult Utah or a cloud generation service. Apple Intelligence must be enabled and its required model downloaded for new readings.

Purchases

Apple processes optional consumable reading-credit purchases through StoreKit. Veil stores verified transaction identifiers, credit grants, refund state, and reading debit identifiers in a device-only Keychain wallet to track your balance and prevent duplicate credits. Consult Utah does not receive your payment card details. Credits are not synced through a Veil account, server, or iCloud wallet. Device loss, erasure, or loss of the Keychain wallet can result in loss of remaining credits; reinstall recovery is not guaranteed.

Sharing and permissions

The camera is used only when you choose to take a palm photo. You can select a photo from your library instead. If you choose to share a reading, the destination you select receives the shared content and its own privacy practices apply. Manage camera access in iOS Settings.

Tracking

Veil has no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Apple may process purchase, platform, or diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings.

Retention and your choices

Reading history stays on your device until removed through available app controls or by deleting the app and its local data. Device-only Keychain purchase records may persist after app deletion. Veil does not provide a cloud backup of your credit balance. Contact us for questions about local data removal; we cannot retrieve palm photos or readings from a Veil server.

Support

If you email us, we receive what you choose to send, such as your email address, device details, or screenshots, and use it to respond to your request. Avoid sending palm photos unless you intend to share them. Support messages may be retained as needed to resolve the issue or meet legal obligations.
