ClosingReels Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026. ClosingReels is provided by Consult Utah.

Your photos and videos stay on your device

ClosingReels stores your selected photos, listing details, narration text, drafts, generated videos, and free-export count locally on your iPhone. Consult Utah does not receive these files or operate an app server to process them. Video rendering and speech generation run on your device. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Services you choose to use

Downloading the natural voice pack connects to GitHub-hosted files. That provider receives ordinary network information such as your IP address. Optional Zillow listing import and the embedded listing browser contact Zillow and websites or resources loaded in that browser directly; their privacy policies and cookies apply. Your narration is not sent to a speech service.

Purchases and Restore Purchases are handled by Apple. ClosingReels uses Apple-verified purchase information on your device to enable the lifetime unlock. Consult Utah does not receive your payment card details.

Sharing and backups

Saving or sharing a video sends it only to the destination you select. That service's privacy practices apply. Your device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Retention and your choices

You can delete saved reels in the app. Deleting a reel does not reset the locally stored free-export count. Deleting the app removes its local data; backups and copies you exported are managed separately. Apple's purchase records are managed by Apple.

Support

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. We do not sell your personal information. For support, privacy questions, or requests concerning support correspondence, contact jeff@consultutah.com.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date if these practices change.
